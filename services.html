Our Services

Everything it takes to turn your data into decisions and automated action

Digital Solutions Consulting is a data and automation company. We take your data from source to action: engineering the foundations, building the analytics, applying AI, automating the work around it, and delivering it through products your teams use every day. Agile, simple, and adaptable.

01

Data Engineering & Platforms

The foundation of every data-driven business. We design and build the pipelines, databases, and cloud platforms that bring your data together, keep it clean, and make it available when and where it's needed.

Services Offered:

  • ETL/ELT Pipelines

    Automated, monitored data pipelines that ingest, clean, and transform data from every source

  • Data Warehousing & Modelling

    Well-modelled warehouses and data marts that give every team one trusted version of the truth

  • Database Design

    RDBMS, cloud-based, and big data solutions optimized for performance and scalability

  • Cloud Data Platforms

    Azure-based data architecture that scales with your data volumes and budget

  • Web Scraping & Data Integration

    Automated data collection and integration from external sources, APIs, and legacy systems

Key Benefits:

Single source of truth Reliable pipelines Clean, trusted data Scalable infrastructure Less manual work
