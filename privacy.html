Privacy Policy

How we collect, use, and protect your personal information

Last updated: 8 August 2026

1. Who We Are

This Privacy Policy explains how Digital Solutions Consulting ("DSC", "we", "us", "our"), a consultancy based in Cape Town, South Africa, collects, uses, and protects personal information submitted through www.digitalsolutionsconsulting.co.za (the "Site"). We process personal information in accordance with South Africa's Protection of Personal Information Act, 2013 (POPIA).

If you have questions about this policy or how your information is handled, contact us at info@dscsa.co.za.

2. Information We Collect

We only collect personal information that you choose to submit to us. The Site's contact form asks for:

  • Full name
  • Email address
  • Company name (optional)
  • Phone number (optional)
  • Service you're interested in
  • Project budget range and timeline (optional)
  • Project details / message content

We do not use cookies, session storage, or any analytics/tracking scripts on this Site, and we do not collect information passively as you browse.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the information you submit solely to respond to your enquiry, discuss your project requirements, and provide you with a quote or proposal. We do not sell, rent, or share your information with third parties for marketing purposes.

4. Third-Party Services

To operate the Site, we rely on a small number of third-party service providers who process data on our behalf:

  • Web3Forms — processes and delivers contact form submissions to our inbox. Form data is transmitted directly to Web3Forms' servers when you submit the form; see Web3Forms' own privacy policy for details on their handling of that data.
  • Google Fonts — our web font is loaded from Google's font CDN (fonts.googleapis.com / fonts.gstatic.com), which may log standard technical request data (such as IP address) as part of serving the font files.

5. Data Retention

We retain contact form submissions only for as long as necessary to respond to your enquiry and manage any resulting engagement, after which they are deleted unless we are required to keep records for legal, accounting, or contractual reasons.

6. Your Rights

Under POPIA, you have the right to:

  • Request confirmation of whether we hold personal information about you
  • Request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information
  • Object to the processing of your personal information
  • Lodge a complaint with the Information Regulator of South Africa

To exercise any of these rights, email us at info@dscsa.co.za and we'll respond within a reasonable time.

7. Security

We take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect the personal information we receive against loss, misuse, and unauthorised access. No method of electronic transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.
