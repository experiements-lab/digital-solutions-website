About Digital Solutions Consulting

A data and automation company, engineering clarity from complexity since day one

Who We Are

Digital Solutions Consulting (DSC) is a boutique data and automation company based in Cape Town, South Africa. We help organisations collect, organise, analyse, and act on their data, from engineering the platforms underneath to the analytics, AI, and automation that put it to work.

Founded by Nando Bingani, a seasoned data engineer and AI specialist, we bring multi-industry data expertise to every engagement. We believe data is only valuable when it changes a decision, and every hour of manual work is worth automating, so we pair technical depth with business understanding, delivering data solutions that are not just technically sound but strategically aligned with your goals.
